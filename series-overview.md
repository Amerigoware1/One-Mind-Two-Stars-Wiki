# The definitive guide to the sci-fi series by Emmery Chrisco

<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Series Metadata

**Genre:** Speculative Hard Sci-Fi / Character-Driven Space Opera  
**Target Audience:** Adult / YA Crossover  
**Core Themes:** Autonomy, Neurodiversity, First Contact Ethics, Found Family, Sustainable Systems  
**Series Length:** Nine-book generational ennealogy (three connected trilogies spanning ~60 years)  

</div> <div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Series Logline

> A girl whose single consciousness spans two bodies becomes the first bridge between humanity and a hidden alien collective—sparking a multi-generational legacy that reshapes both species across sixty years on the moon Ares.

</div> <div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Book-by-Book Breakdown

### Arc I: One Mind, Two Stars (2232–2246)
* **Book 1: The Bridge of Ares (2232)** – 7-year-old Celectra Carisco, possessing one mind across two bodies, flees an unethical chief of medicine into the subterranean caves of Ares, discovering the liquid-metal Galinstanians and becoming a bridge between species [📖 Book 1 Wiki]({{ '/book1.html' | relative_url }}) | [✍️ Sample Chapter]({{ '/book1samplechapter.html' | relative_url }}).
* **Book 2: The Northern Shadows (2239) & Book 3: Neutron Legacy (2246)** – Celectra navigates the exhaustion of being New Eden's sole translator, confronts a rogue researcher exploiting Galinstanians, and eventually trains a "Listener Corps" as she transitions toward technological alternatives and motherhood.

### Arc II: Two Stars, One Destiny (2255–2269)
* **Book 4: The Four Rivers Resonance (2255)** – Celectra's twin daughters, Jenni Jo and Xara Zo, discover human infrastructure is disrupting the Galinstanian subsurface network and help orchestrate a harmonic restoration.
* **Book 5: The Resonant Echo (2262) & Book 6: Polar Opposites (2269)** – The twins rescue a sealed Galinstanian collective from a museum wall and lead separate polar expeditions to uncover ancient geological history and long-isolated survivors.

### Arc III: One Journey, Many Ways (2277–2291)
* **Books 7–9 (2277–2291)** – Eight years after the polar expeditions, Jenni and Xara board the deep-crust mag-lev exploration rover *The Wayfarer* alongside their families and Galinstanian allies to survey Ares's uncharted Memory Veins [🚜 *The Wayfarer* Wiki]({{ '/wayfarer.html' | relative_url }}).
