<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
Emmery Chrisco is a science fiction author, amateur programmer, and builder of speculative systems. Driven by a lifelong fascination with deep space, astrophysics, and orbital mechanics, they treat worldbuilding not just as a narrative backdrop, but as an intricate system to be precisely engineered.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
Emmery's creative process is definingly hands-on and multidisciplinary. When not constructing the underlying HTML, CSS, and directory frameworks for this digital archive, they can be found applying that same love for structure to physical materials—whether crafting custom woodworking projects or designing practical, everyday DIY engineering solutions. Across both digital and physical landscapes, their work reflects a core preoccupation with how systems function and how minds survive, connect, and build meaning in highly regulated environments.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
As a triple-A (ace, aro, agender) creator, Emmery brings a distinct personal lens to the worlds they build. Their work deliberately bypasses conventional storytelling tropes to explore the deep complexities of identity and unconventional connection, establishing universes where diverse realities exist simply as a natural, unquestioned fabric of the future.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
Project Philosophy
Architectural Worldbuilding: Designing high-concept frontiers grounded in rigorous scientific plausibility, from technical space station specifications to detailed planetary physics.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
Integrated Design: Merging narrative prose, technical documentation, and custom web architecture into a unified, living encyclopedia.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">
Casual Representation: Normalizing diverse identities by weaving them seamlessly into the background of a sprawling future, focusing the conflict on survival and exploration rather than the right to exist.
</div>
