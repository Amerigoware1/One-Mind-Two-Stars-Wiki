Overview & Historical Context

The Listener Corps was humanity's foundational auxiliary translation team on Ares. Founded by Celectra Ambar Carisco in 2239, it was established to manage the initial volume of Galinstanian communication. It marked the first ad-hoc phase of cross-species relations, relying entirely on human volunteers trained in mental “unfocusing” techniques to interpret passive electromagnetic impressions.

Though initially limited by biological constraints—as only Celectra possessed the bidirectional capability to broadcast back into the bismuth network—the Corps laid the social and organizational groundwork for all future integration protocols.

The Expansion & Technological Transition (2246–2248)

By 2246, the program scaled up significantly to meet growing operational footprints, expanding to over 50 members and volunteers across the four major river settlements (new-eden, gihon, hiddekel, and euphrates). This rapid growth exposed the limitations of relying on a single human bridge.

Following Celectra's completion of her Ph.D. in 2246, the historical paradigm of passive listening was completely transformed by the development of the Artificial Neural Interface (a.n.i.). The Corps shifted from a passive reception team into an active interface department of certified headset operators, field engineers, and advanced negotiators.

Evolution into Resonance Stewardship (2255)

On January 28, 2255, the Ares Council voted unanimously to permanently transition the department infrastructure into the Resonance Stewardship Program. With the A.N.I. standardizing day-to-day language translation, the joint human–Galinstanian initiative shifted focus from raw translation to monitoring, protecting, and studying the physical and harmonic health of the subsurface bismuth network and planetary crust.
