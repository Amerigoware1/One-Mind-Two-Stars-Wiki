Wetsuit‑Integrated Artificial Gill

The primary challenge in designing a compact artificial gill is achieving the enormous membrane surface area (estimated-at 60–100 m²) required for sufficient oxygen transfer, all within a wearable form factor. This design integrates the Hollow Fiber Membrane (hfm) module directly into the back panel of a wetsuit, forming a streamlined dorsal extension approximately 14 cm thick. This configuration allows for high‑efficiency gas exchange without impeding swimming motion or requiring external gear.
