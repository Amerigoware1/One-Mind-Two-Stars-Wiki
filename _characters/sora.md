## Overview
Sora Vongphachanh is the firstborn child of Mei Vongphachanh and Samir, and the eldest grandchild of Aulii Vongphachanh. Born in 2270, Sora grows up almost entirely aboard *The Wayfarer* during the Global Planetary Survey. As Jasper Aoki-Carisco's exact age and closest wilderness cohort, Sora provides a boisterous, physical, and grounding counterweight to Jasper's internalized sensory processing. Where Jasper hears the music of the planet, Sora feels the ground beneath his feet. He represents the next generation's physical adaptation to the Ares wilderness: agile, fiercely loyal, and entirely at home in the wild.

* career_title: "Wilderness Scout / Field Operations Specialist"

  - Expedition logistics (under Samir's mentorship)

## Physical Description
Sora is a vibrant blend of his family's lineages. He has light-medium warm skin (4) with a sun-kissed undertone from years spent in the rover's observation decks and on surface excursions. His hair is very dark brown (8), thick, and falls in loose, manageable waves that he often keeps tied back in a short, practical tail. His eyes are dark brown and almond-shaped, carrying the subtle epicanthic fold inherited from his Southeast Asian heritage, but they hold a bright, mischievous, and highly observant spark. He has an athletic, wiry build, shaped by climbing canyon walls and navigating uneven terrain. Like his father Samir and grandmother Aulii, Sora is almost always barefoot indoors, preferring to feel the subtle vibrations of the rover's mag-lev floor and the planet's crust.
</div>

## Personality & Skills
Sora is defined by his **physical intuition, boisterous loyalty, and adaptable resilience**. He is the action-oriented counterpart to the more introspective members of the expedition.

His strengths include:
* **Environmental Agility:** Unmatched ability to navigate treacherous, unmapped wilderness terrain, scaling cliffs and descending into canyons with instinctive grace.
* **Pack Loyalty:** Fiercely protective of his "pack" (Jasper, Nia, and Jasmine). He is the first to step in when his friends are overwhelmed or in danger.
* **Practical Problem-Solving:** While others analyze the resonance, Sora finds the physical workaround. He is a master of the "Butterfly" drone tethering systems.
* **Infectious Optimism:** His bright, unfiltered energy keeps morale high during long, grueling wilderness transits.

His weaknesses include:
* Impulsivity; he often acts before fully assessing the harmonic risks of an environment.
* Struggles with the quiet, prolonged introspection required for deep lithic translation, occasionally frustrating his grandmother Aulii.
* Tends to mask his own emotional overwhelm with physical activity or humor.
</div>

## Relationships
* **Mei Vongphachanh** — biological mother ("Mama"). Sora inherits her warmth and pragmatic nurturing, though he expresses it through action rather than cultivation.
* **Samir** — adoptive father ("Papa"). Sora idolizes Samir's piloting skills and quiet competence, often sitting in the cockpit for hours just watching him work the controls.
* **Aulii Vongphachanh** — grandmother. Aulii is his fiercest protector and most demanding teacher. She is patiently teaching him that true strength requires stillness, not just motion.
* **Jasper Aoki-Carisco** — best friend and exact-age cohort. Their dynamic is legendary: Sora is the physical anchor who pulls Jasper out of channel-conflict freezes, while Jasper helps Sora understand the deeper patterns of the environments they explore.
* **Nia Vongphachanh** — younger sister. Sora is fiercely protective of Nia, though he frequently teases her. They share a deep, unspoken sibling bond forged in the confined space of the rover.
* **Jasmine Carisco-Vance** — cousin and friend. Sora respects Jasmine's calm, anchoring presence and often defers to her judgment when the environment becomes harmonically unstable.
</div>

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Sora's arc is about learning that true strength is not just about physical endurance or being the fastest climber in the canyon. Growing up in the back of a massive mag-lev rover, he initially views the wilderness as a playground to be conquered. However, as the expedition pushes into deeper, more dangerous harmonic zones, Sora must learn the value of his grandmother's "stillness protocol." His journey is about integrating his boundless physical energy with the quiet, receptive awareness needed to truly survive—and thrive—in the wild heart of Ares.

## Themes
* **Physicality vs. Stillness:** Learning that boundless energy must be balanced with receptive quiet to survive the deep wilderness.
* **The Wilderness as Home:** Redefining "home" not as a static colony dome, but as the moving, breathing ecosystem of *The Wayfarer* and the Ares landscape.
* **Pack Loyalty:** The idea that family is a chosen, fiercely protected unit that relies on each member's unique strengths.
* **Action as Love:** Expressing care and protection through physical presence and decisive action rather than words.

## Trivia
* Sora learned to scale the interior cargo nets of *The Wayfarer* before he could properly read, much to Samir's amusement and Mei's mild exasperation.
* He is almost always barefoot, a habit he shares with his father and grandmother, claiming he can "feel the mag-lev hum" and tell if the rover is running smoothly.
* He and Jasper have a secret handshake that involves a complex series of rhythmic taps, blending Sora's physical agility with Jasper's acoustic precision.
* Despite his boisterous nature, Sora is the only person who can get his grandmother Aulii to break her "stillness protocol" and laugh out loud, usually by attempting (and failing) to mimic her perfect martial stances.
* He keeps a collection of smooth, non-resonant river stones in his pockets, which he unconsciously rubs when he is trying to focus or calm his own impulsivity.
