## Overview
Nia Vongphachanh is the younger daughter of Mei Vongphachanh and Samir, and the younger granddaughter of Aulii Vongphachanh. Born in 2272, she grows up entirely aboard *The Wayfarer* during the Global Planetary Survey. While her older brother Sora is the physical anchor and her cousin Jasmine is the harmonic stabilizer, Nia is the "Harmonic Mirror"—an empathic archivist who can perfectly mimic and reflect the emotional and resonance states of the environments and beings they encounter. She is Jasmine's closest confidante and playful companion, bringing curiosity, joy, and a profound ability to record the "song" of Ares.

* career_title: "Resonance Archivist / Emotional Historian"
* education: >
  Self-paced education under Utara's satellite curriculum. Focus areas:
  - Acoustic mimicry and harmonic mirroring
  - Empathic recording and emotional preservation
  - Historical documentation and Galinstanian interface protocols
  - Diplomatic liaison training
  By Book 9, Nia has established Resonance Archiving as a formal Stewardship
  discipline—the second new field created by the second generation (after Jasper's
  Resonance Composition).

## Physical Description
Nia is a vibrant blend of her family's lineages. She has light olive skin (4) with a warm, sun-kissed undertone. Her hair is very dark brown (8), thick, and falls in loose, bouncy curls that she often wears in two practical braids or a messy half-up style. Her eyes are dark brown and almond-shaped, carrying the subtle epicanthic fold inherited from her grandmother Aulii, but they sparkle with a mischievous, intensely curious light. She has a slender, agile build, shaped by years of climbing around the rover's interior and exploring surface outcroppings. Like her family, she is almost always barefoot indoors, claiming she can "hear the water moving" through the rover's pipes.

## Personality & Skills
Nia is defined by her **boundless curiosity, empathic reflection, and joyful adaptability**. She is the emotional historian of the expedition.

Her strengths include:
* **Harmonic Mirroring:** Can perfectly mimic and reflect the emotional and resonance states of her environment, allowing her to "record" the song of a place or person.
* **Empathic Archiving:** Uses her mirroring ability to create detailed, emotional audio-resonance logs of the expedition, preserving the history of the Galinstanian clusters they meet.
* **Social Bridge:** Her joyful, unfiltered nature puts nervous or isolated beings at ease; she is the expedition's unofficial diplomat to younger or more timid Galinstanians.
* **Acoustic Mimicry:** Can perfectly replicate the multi-tone taps and acoustic rhythms of the Galinstanians, often playing call-and-response games with Ripple.

Her weaknesses include:
* Can become temporarily overwhelmed if she mirrors a highly chaotic or painful resonance field without Jasmine's anchoring presence.
* Her playful nature can sometimes mask a deep sensitivity to the emotional weight of the wilderness.
</div>

## Relationships
* **Mei Vongphachanh** — biological mother ("Mama"). Nia inherits her mother's warmth and pragmatic nurturing, expressing it through joyful exploration.
* **Samir** — adoptive father ("Papa"). Nia idolizes Samir's piloting skills and often sits in the co-pilot seat, "helping" him navigate by calling out resonance shifts she hears.
* **Aulii Vongphachanh** — grandmother. Aulii is her fiercest protector. Nia is the one who successfully coaxes Aulii into smiling, often by mimicking her grandmother's precise martial stances with exaggerated, playful flair.
* **Sora Vongphachanh** — older brother. Nia looks up to Sora's physical bravery, though she frequently teases him for being too serious. They share a deep, protective sibling bond.
* **Jasmine Carisco-Vance** — cousin and closest confidante. Where Jasmine anchors the chaos, Nia reflects it. They are inseparable, with Nia often using her mirroring to help Jasmine process the emotional weight she absorbs.
* **Jasper Aoki-Carisco** — cousin. Nia and Jasper often collaborate, with Jasper weaving the acoustic rhythms and Nia mirroring the emotional textures, creating beautiful, impromptu duets in the rover's cargo bay.
* **Diomid** — biological father (off-page). A respected hydroponics engineer in New Eden who sends her regular, cheerful audio-messages, which she lovingly archives alongside her expedition logs.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Nia's arc is about the power of joyful witness. Growing up in the back of a massive mag-lev rover, she views the Ares wilderness not as a dangerous frontier to be conquered, but as a grand, beautiful song to be learned and recorded. Her journey is about discovering that her ability to mirror the world's emotions is not just a parlor trick, but a vital historical and diplomatic tool. By the end of the expedition, Nia becomes the expedition's official Resonance Archivist, ensuring that the history of the Galinstanian clusters is preserved not just in data, but in feeling.

## Themes
* **Joyful Witness:** The idea that observing and recording the world with joy and curiosity is a profound and necessary act.
* **The Harmonic Mirror:** Empathy not as a burden to be carried, but as a reflection to be shared and understood.
* **The Emotional Archive:** Preserving history not just in facts and data, but in the emotional and acoustic textures of the moments.
* **Chosen Family:** The deep, unbreakable bond of a multi-generational, non-traditional family navigating the wilderness together.
