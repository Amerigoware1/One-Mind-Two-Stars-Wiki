## Overview
Tara Hui Chentel is the firstborn daughter of Ananya and Chen Wei Chentel. Conceived late in Book 3, she grows up immersed in the scientific and diplomatic efforts of New Eden. Practical, observant, and deeply rooted in the colony's community, she eventually builds her own family, continuing the legacy of integration and stewardship.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Physical Description
Tara inherits her mother's warm brown skin and dark hair, which she often wears tied back to keep it out of the way during her many curious explorations of the colony. She has the observant, focused eyes of her father, giving her a look of constant, quiet assessment.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Personality & Skills
Tara is defined by:
- **innate curiosity**
- **practical problem-solving**
- **grounded stability**
- **observational intelligence**
Her strengths include:
- asking insightful questions
- adapting to new environments
- looking out for her younger siblings
Her weaknesses include:
- occasional stubbornness
- taking on too much responsibility at a young age
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Relationships
- **Ananya Chentel** — mother; Tara deeply admires her scientific discipline and straightforward nature.
- **Chen Wei Chentel** — father; she shares his analytical mindset and enjoys learning about his technical work.
- **Jia Kiran Chentel** — younger sister; they share a close, protective bond.
- **Aditi Ming Chentel** — youngest brother; Tara is fiercely protective of him.
- **Celectra Carisco** — honorary aunt; Tara has grown up knowing Celectra as a warm family friend and hero of the colony.
- **Her own children** — By Book 9, Tara is a mother, raising her own kids in New Eden.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Tara's story is one of legacy and continuity. Born to two key architects of human–Galinstanian communication, she grows up in the shadow of—and eventually alongside—their achievements. Her arc shifts from a curious child observing the adults' work to an independent adult starting a family of her own, proving that the systems her parents built will last for generations.
