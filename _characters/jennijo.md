## Overview
Dr. Jenni Jo Carisco-Vance (née Carisco) is the older of Celectra's twin daughters and Ares' foremost Resonance Psychologist. At seven years old in Book 4, she experiences resonance as a feeling — warmth, pressure, brightness, unease — long before she can articulate the underlying patterns. By twenty-one, her arc shifts from raw, unfiltered empathy to profound, deliberate stabilization. 

Following in the psychological footsteps of her grandmother, Dr. Zoë Carisco, Jenni earns her PsyD to specialize in the mental and emotional fallout of resonance events. As the romantic partner of Dr. Elara Vance and mother to Jasmine, Jenni evolves into a leader who bridges the gap between human emotion, psychological health, and Galinstanian flow. She is the "heart" to Xara's "mind," forming the emotional and psychological bedrock of the colony's next generation.

## Physical Description
Jenni stands at 158 cm with a slender, graceful build. She has medium brown skin (6) with a warm undertone, and dark brown (8), thick, voluminous curly hair that she often pulls back or styles with bismuth-thread beads. Her dark brown, almond-shaped eyes are rich, expressive, and immediately convey her emotional state. 

She moves with a soft, instinctive awareness of her surroundings, often leaning into resonance rather than analyzing it. Her posture is relaxed, her gestures vivid and unfiltered. By Book 6, she favors a youthful take on Listener Corps practical attire accented with bismuth-thread weaving. Like many in her family, she rarely wears shoes indoors, preferring to feel the subtle, emotional vibrations of the colony's architecture through the soles of her feet.

## Personality & Skills
Jenni is defined by her **evolved emotional intelligence, instinctive resonance sensitivity, and warm, protective openness**. She has transitioned from a "feel first, think second" teenager to a mature Resonance Psychologist who knows how to channel her vast empathy without losing herself.

Her strengths include:
*   **Resonance Psychology (PsyD):** Expert in diagnosing and treating ANI trauma, sensory overload, "resonance sickness," and the psychological toll of harmonic biomes.
*   **Empathic Stabilization:** Unparalleled ability to detect emotional-harmonic shifts and deliver calming pulses during resonance instability.
*   **Intuitive Bridging:** A natural, effortless understanding of Galinstanian emotional fields, allowing her to communicate with them on a level pure data cannot reach.
*   **Emotional Courage:** Willingness to step into chaotic, painful resonance fields to anchor others, protected by the grounding routines her partner, Elara, teaches her.

Her weaknesses include:
*   Occasional lingering tendency to prioritize emotion over structural analysis (though she now relies on Elara to bridge this gap).
*   Can still struggle to articulate the depth of what she intuitively perceives, requiring patience from those who don't "feel" the music the way she does.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Jenni's arc centers on the evolution of empathy into science. As a child, she was a sponge for the world's feelings, often overwhelmed by the emotional weight of the colony and the Galinstanians. Her journey is about learning that empathy is not just about feeling what others feel; it is about having the psychological training and personal boundaries to hold that weight and transform it into stability. By earning her PsyD, partnering with Elara, and raising Jasmine, Jenni proves that the "heart" of the Stewardship Program is not a weakness to be managed, but the very engine of their survival.

*   **First Pulses (Book 4):** At age seven, provides crucial stabilizing emotional pulses during the Great Resonance, proving her innate sensitivity.
*   **Junior Steward (Book 5):** Joins the Resonance Stewardship Program, specializing in emotional-harmonic modulation and deepening her bond with Ripple.
*   **The Northern Pole Crisis (Book 6):** At the Northern Pole, her intuitive feelings detect the root of a harmonic anomaly that defies Xara's data. She trusts her instincts, allowing Elara to translate her feelings into a colony-saving protocol.
*   **The Harmonic Conception (Book 6):** She and Elara conceive their daughter, Jasmine, via the Harmonic IVG Protocol, blending their emotional and scientific legacies into a new life.
*   **The PsyD Achievement (Book 7):** Officially earns her doctorate in Resonance Psychology, following in her grandmother Zoë's footsteps, and joins the Global Survey Team to study the psychological impacts of the Ares wilderness.
*   **The Empathic Framework (Book 8):** Co-authors the new stewardship protocols that officially integrate emotional intelligence and psychological resilience into the colony's hard science.
