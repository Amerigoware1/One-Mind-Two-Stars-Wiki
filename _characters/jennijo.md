## Overview
Jenni Jo Carisco is the older of Celectra's twin daughters and one of the most emotionally attuned listeners on Ares. At seven, she experiences resonance as a feeling — warmth, pressure, brightness, unease — long before she can articulate the underlying patterns. By twenty-one, her arc shifts from raw, unfiltered empathy to profound, deliberate stabilization. 

As the romantic partner of Dr. Elara Vance and mother to Jasmine, Jenni evolves into a leader who bridges the gap between human emotion and Galinstanian flow. She is the "heart" to Xara's "mind," and together with Elara's "structure," she forms the emotional bedrock of the colony's next generation.

</div>

## Physical Description
Jenni stands at 158 cm with a slender, graceful build. She has medium brown skin (6) with a warm undertone, and dark brown (8), thick, voluminous curly hair that she often pulls back or styles with bismuth-thread beads. Her dark brown, almond-shaped eyes are rich, expressive, and immediately convey her emotional state. 

She moves with a soft, instinctive awareness of her surroundings, often leaning into resonance rather than analyzing it. Her posture is relaxed, her gestures vivid and unfiltered. By Book 6, she favors a youthful take on Listener Corps practical attire accented with bismuth-thread weaving. Like many in her family, she rarely wears shoes indoors, preferring to feel the subtle, emotional vibrations of the colony's architecture through the soles of her feet.

## Personality & Skills
Jenni is defined by her **evolved emotional intelligence, instinctive resonance sensitivity, and warm, protective openness**. She has transitioned from a "feel first, think second" teenager to a mature stabilizer who knows how to channel her vast empathy without losing herself.

Her strengths include:
*   **Empathic Stabilization:** Unparalleled ability to detect emotional-harmonic shifts and deliver calming pulses during resonance instability.
*   **Intuitive Bridging:** A natural, effortless understanding of Galinstanian emotional fields, allowing her to communicate with them on a level pure data cannot reach.
*   **Emotional Courage:** Willingness to step into chaotic, painful resonance fields to anchor others, protected by the grounding routines her partner, Elara, teaches her.

Her weaknesses include:
*   Occasional lingering tendency to prioritize emotion over structural analysis (though she now relies on Elara to bridge this gap).
*   Can still struggle to articulate the depth of what she intuitively perceives, requiring patience from those who don't "feel" the music the way she does.

## Relationships
*   **Dr. Elara Vance** — romantic partner and wife; the "structure" to her "flow." Elara provides the grounded, analytical container that keeps Jenni's expansive emotions safe, while Jenni brings color, warmth, and spontaneous joy into Elara’s highly structured life.
*   **Jasmine Carisco-Vance** — daughter; conceived via IVG, Jasmine inherited Jenni's expressive warmth and deep empathic connection, balanced by Elara's striking amber eyes and calm observation.

*   **Kael Aoki & Jasper Aoki-Carisco** — brother-in-law and nephew; Jenni adores Jasper's dual-channel perception and often plays acoustic games with him, helping him ground his overwhelming sensory input.
*   **Ripple** — closest friend and emotional partner; their bond transcends typical human–Galinstanian connection, serving as the foundation for Jenni's entire stewardship philosophy.
*   **Celectra & Jaxon** — parents; Jenni's sensitivity mirrors Celectra's, while her steadiness and physical grounding come from Jaxon.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Jenni's arc centers on the evolution of empathy. As a child, she was a sponge for the world's feelings, often overwhelmed by the emotional weight of the colony and the Galinstanians. Her journey is about learning that empathy is not just about feeling what others feel; it is about having the strength to hold that weight and transform it into stability. By partnering with Elara and raising Jasmine, Jenni proves that the "heart" of the Stewardship Program is not a weakness to be managed, but the very engine of their survival.
