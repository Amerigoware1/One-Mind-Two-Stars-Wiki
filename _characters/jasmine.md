## Overview
Jasmine Carisco-Vance is the daughter of Dr. Jenni Jo Carisco-Vance and Dr. Elara Vance, conceived via Elara's groundbreaking Harmonic IVG Protocol at the close of Book 6. She is the first true "resonance baby" — a child whose genetic and harmonic development was stabilized from the first cell division by Ares' ambient resonance fields. Where her mother Jenni feels the world's emotional weight and her co-mother Elara builds the scientific frameworks to understand it, Jasmine naturally embodies the balance of both. She is a living harmonic anchor, possessing an innate bio-harmonic presence that calms chaotic EM fields simply by her proximity. Her arc across the final trilogy is not about learning to use her gift, but about learning to protect her own boundaries while generously sharing her stabilizing presence with a colony — and eventually a planet — that increasingly relies on her.

## Physical Description
Jasmine is a stunning, harmonious blend of her mothers' phenotypes. She has medium olive skin (5) with a warm, radiant undertone, perfectly adapted to the Ares environment. Her hair is very dark brown (8), thick, and falls in voluminous, springy ringlets that combine Jenni's lush curl pattern with Elara's heavy wave definition. Her most striking and defining feature is her eyes: a luminous, warm amber inherited directly from Elara. In a family of dark-eyed Cariscos, Jasmine's amber eyes give her a unique visual identity and an intensely perceptive, "old soul" gaze that seems to understand the song of Ares long before she can articulate it. She moves with a quiet, grounded grace that contrasts beautifully with Jenni's vivid expressiveness, and like her mothers, she rarely wears shoes indoors, preferring to feel the harmonic vibrations of the world through the soles of her feet.
</div>

## Personality & Skills
Jasmine is defined by her **innate calm, deep observational empathy, and harmonic grounding**. Unlike Jenni, who can be overwhelmed by ambient emotion, Jasmine processes and regulates harmonic weight with remarkable stability — a trait that both amazes and occasionally worries her mothers.

Her strengths include:
* **Harmonic Anchoring:** An innate, biological ability to naturally settle chaotic EM fields and soothe resonance spikes in both people and environments.
* **Emotional Regulation:** Processes ambient harmonic weight without absorbing it; can remain centered in chaotic fields that would overwhelm adults.
* **Intuitive Bridging:** A natural, effortless understanding of both human emotional states and Galinstanian flow patterns.
* **Quiet Observation:** Watches and listens before acting; her amber eyes miss very little.

Her weaknesses include:
* Can unconsciously absorb too much ambient stress if she doesn't actively practice the grounding techniques her mothers teach her.
* Sometimes struggles to articulate the depth of what she feels, as harmonic stability is so "normal" to her that she forgets others experience it as chaos.
* Carries the weight of being a "resonance baby" — the living proof of her mothers' life work — and occasionally resents the expectations that come with it.
</div>

## Relationships
* **Dr. Jenni Jo Carisco-Vance** — mother ("Mama"); Jenni's boundless warmth and emotional intuition are the foundation of Jasmine's secure, loving upbringing. Jasmine calls her "Mama" and runs to her first for emotional comfort.
* **Dr. Elara Vance** — co-mother ("Mom"); Elara's scientific rigor and protective grounding ensure Jasmine's unique abilities are nurtured safely and ethically. Jasmine calls her "Mom" and goes to her first for structural or scientific questions.
* **Xara Zo Carisco** — aunt; Xara adores her niece and often collaborates with her, marveling at how Jasmine can "feel the math" in a way that perfectly complements Xara's analytical mapping.
* **Kael Aoki** — uncle; Kael teaches her to listen to the downbeat of a field, and she teaches him that some fields don't need rhythm — they just need stillness.
* **Jasper Aoki-Carisco** — cousin and closest peer; their rivalry is legendary. Jasper claims rhythm can fix anything; Jasmine claims presence can. They have been settling it with demonstrations since they were six. Where Jasper hums and weaves acoustic rhythms, Jasmine provides the calm, steady foundation for his music to flourish.
* **Sora & Nia** — fellow rover-born children; Jasmine and Nia share a curious, observant kinship, while Sora and Jasper form a more boisterous, physical pairing.
* **Ripple** — Galinstanian family friend and mentor; Ripple refers to Jasmine affectionately as "Little Stillness" or "The Quiet Heart" and trusts her implicitly, recognizing her as a natural bridge between their species.
* **Aulii Vongphachanh** — expedition elder; Aulii teaches Jasmine the martial art of internal stillness, the physical discipline that underlies her harmonic anchoring.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Jasmine's arc is about growing up as a living symbol of human-Galinstanian harmony. Born from a revolutionary scientific and emotional partnership, she must learn to navigate the expectations placed upon her as a "resonance prodigy." Her journey is not about learning how to use her powers — she has had them since birth — but about learning how to protect her own boundaries while generously sharing her gift of stability with a colony, and eventually a planet, that increasingly relies on her unique presence. Alongside her cousin Jasper, she represents the next evolution of human-Galinstanian integration: a generation for whom the harmony is not a challenge to overcome, but a natural, beautiful reality to inherit and refine.
</div>

## Key Moments
* **The Harmonic Conception (Book 6):** Conceived via Elara's IVG protocol, stabilized from the first cell division by Ares' ambient resonance, marking a new era in bio-harmonic science.
* **The First Anchor (Book 7):** As a young child, Jasmine unconsciously calms a severe resonance spike in the Activity Center, drawing the attention of the Stewardship elders and confirming her innate gift.
* **The "Mommy!" Sprint (Book 7):** As a toddler, she frequently yells "Mommy!" in times of crisis or excitement, resulting in a chaotic, loving sprint from both Jenni and Elara, who inevitably arrive at the exact same time — a domestic ritual that never stops delighting the crew of *The Wayfarer*.
* **The Living Stabilizer (Book 8):** As a teenager, she voluntarily steps into a frayed bismuth network during a colony-wide crisis, acting as a living anchor to prevent a catastrophic harmonic cascade.
* **The New Protocol (Book 9):** Jasmine co-publishes her own bio-harmonic integration frameworks alongside Jasper's composite notation, officially transitioning from a "prodigy" to a respected leader in her own right.
