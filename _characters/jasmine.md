## Overview
Jasmine Carisco-Vance is the daughter of Jenni Jo Carisco-Bowers and Dr. Elara Vance, conceived via the groundbreaking Harmonic IVG Protocol. She is a true "resonance baby," representing the next evolutionary step in human-Galinstanian integration. Where her mother Jenni feels the emotional weight of the world and her co-mother Elara builds the scientific framework to understand it, Jasmine naturally embodies the balance of both. She is a living, breathing stabilizer, possessing an innate bio-harmonic presence that calms chaotic EM fields simply by her proximity.

</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Physical Description
Jasmine is a stunning, harmonious blend of her mothers' phenotypes. She has medium olive skin (5) with a warm, radiant undertone, perfectly adapted to the Ares environment. Her hair is a rich, very dark brown (8), featuring voluminous, springy ringlets that combine Jenni’s lush curl pattern with Elara’s heavy wave definition. Her most striking feature is her eyes: a luminous, warm amber-brown inherited directly from Elara. In a family of dark-eyed Cariscos, Jasmine’s amber eyes give her a unique visual identity and an intensely perceptive, "old soul" gaze. Even as a toddler, her expressions are calm and deeply observant.

</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Personality & Skills
Jasmine is defined by her **innate calm, deep observational empathy, and harmonic grounding**. 
Her strengths include:
*   **Harmonic Anchoring:** An innate, biological ability to naturally settle chaotic EM fields and soothe resonance spikes in both people and environments.
*   **Emotional Regulation:** Unlike Jenni, who can be overwhelmed by ambient emotion, Jasmine processes and regulates harmonic weight with remarkable stability.
*   **Intuitive Bridging:** A natural, effortless understanding of both human emotional states and Galinstanian flow patterns.
Her weaknesses include:
*   Can unconsciously absorb too much ambient stress if she doesn't actively practice grounding techniques.
*   Sometimes struggles to articulate the depth of what she feels, as harmonic stability is so "normal" to her that she forgets others experience it as chaos.
   
</div>

## Relationships
*   **Jenni Jo Carisco-Bowers** — mother; Jenni’s boundless warmth and emotional intuition are the foundation of Jasmine’s secure, loving upbringing.
*   **Dr. Elara Vance** — co-mother; Elara’s scientific rigor and protective grounding ensure Jasmine’s unique abilities are nurtured safely and ethically.
*   **Xara Zo Carisco-Bowers** — aunt; Xara adores her niece and often collaborates with her, marveling at how Jasmine can "feel the math" in a way that perfectly complements Xara’s analytical mapping.
*   **Jasper Aoki-Carisco** — cousin; Jasper and Jasmine share a profound, playful bond. Where Jasper hums and weaves acoustic rhythms, Jasmine provides the calm, steady foundation for his music to flourish.
*   **Ripple** — Galinstanian family friend and mentor; Ripple refers to Jasmine affectionately as "Little Stillness" and trusts her implicitly, recognizing her as a natural bridge between their species.
  
</div>

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Jasmine’s arc is about growing up as a living symbol of human-Galinstanian harmony. Born from a revolutionary scientific and emotional partnership, she must learn to navigate the expectations placed upon her as a "resonance prodigy." Her journey is not about learning how to use her powers, but about learning how to protect her own boundaries while generously sharing her gift of stability with a colony that increasingly relies on her unique presence.

*   **The Living Anchor:** Stability not as a passive state, but as an active, generous, and powerful choice.
*   **Legacy of Harmony:** Carrying forward the best of both parents—emotional intuition and scientific rigor—without being confined by their past struggles.
*   **Boundaries of Empathy:** Learning that to hold space for others, one must first fiercely protect their own inner peace.
