## Overview

Saoirse Echo Keahi-Calder (pronounced SEER-sha) is the firstborn of the fraternal triplets carried by Maya and co-parented by Sam. Named for freedom (Saoirse) and sound (Echo), she inherits her parent Sam's deep love for audio engineering and artistic expression. She grows up running through the Samaya Gallery, becoming a bold, creative force whose fascination with sound eventually develops into a career exploring acoustics, sensory experience, and cross-species artistic expression.

Alongside her two sisters, she chooses to build a communal household in which they raise their children together. Saoirse brings energy, music, and an almost irresistible enthusiasm to their family, even as she learns that not every moment needs a soundtrack.

## Physical Description

Saoirse has Maya's warm Hawaiian complexion and Sam's striking, expressive eyes. Naturally blonde, she often dyes her hair wild colors (blues and purples) to match her vibrant personality. She dresses in eclectic, layered clothing, often adorned with audio equipment or sonic jewelry.

Her style is expressive rather than coordinated: unusual textures, bold accessories, and the occasional piece of wearable technology that doubles as an experimental instrument. Even when dressed professionally, she rarely looks entirely conventional.

## Personality & Skills

Saoirse is defined by:

- expressive, chaotic energy
- musical intuition
- fierce protectiveness
- unapologetic individuality
- an instinctive awareness of rhythm and atmosphere

Her strengths include:

- audio engineering, sound design, and composition
- acoustic analysis and environmental sound mapping
- emotional communication through sound
- designing immersive and interactive sensory experiences
- translating abstract scientific ideas into engaging artistic experiences
- creative collaboration and unconventional problem-solving

Her weaknesses include:

- impatience
- occasional sensory overload
- struggling to sit still in quiet environments
- becoming so absorbed in a project that she forgets practical responsibilities
- sometimes assuming that everyone enjoys the same level of noise and stimulation she does

Saoirse's greatest artistic strength is her ability to make sound feel alive. She understands that an acoustic environment is more than a collection of frequencies: it can influence mood, attention, memory, and the way people relate to one another.

## Career & Research

Saoirse develops her early enthusiasm for music production into a career as an Acoustic Ecologist and Sonic Experience Designer. Her work combines audio engineering, environmental acoustics, artistic composition, and the study of how different forms of life perceive vibration and sound.

Her interests include:

- Acoustic ecology: studying how sound behaves within natural and artificial environments, including geothermal caves, colony habitats, public spaces, and research facilities.
- Environmental sound design: creating soundscapes that improve the atmosphere and usability of shared spaces without overwhelming their occupants.
- Sensory accessibility: designing audio and multisensory experiences that accommodate different hearing abilities, sensory preferences, and perceptual systems.
- Cross-species resonance: investigating how Galinstanians detect and interpret mechanical vibrations, resonant patterns, and other forms of physical signaling.
- Interactive installations: combining sound, light, vibration, and responsive technology to create experiences that can be explored by humans and Galinstanians alike.

Saoirse is particularly interested in the distinction between hearing a sound and perceiving a pattern. Human listeners may experience a composition as melody, rhythm, and harmony, while a Galinstanian might respond to its mechanical vibrations or structural resonance in an entirely different way. Rather than assuming that the two species experience the same thing, she works with Galinstanian collaborators to discover which patterns are meaningful to them.

Her collaborations with Indigo bring together acoustic design and established ANI technology. Indigo works on the communication systems themselves; Saoirse explores how sound, vibration, and other sensory elements can enrich the experiences those systems make possible. Their installations can combine live performance, interactive visual elements, vibration-sensitive surfaces, and ANI-mediated participation.

By Book 9, Saoirse has become a recognized figure in New Eden's acoustic arts community. Her projects occupy an unusual space between scientific research, public art, and cross-species cultural exchange. She continues to compose music independently, but increasingly sees her work as a way to help different forms of life experience and appreciate one another's worlds.

## Family & Co-Parenting

Saoirse, Kalea, and Indigo make their childhood promise a permanent part of their adult lives. They establish a shared home and raise their children collectively, treating all three as parents rather than assigning rigid divisions of responsibility.

The sisters organize their professional schedules around a rotating home-anchor arrangement, ensuring that at least one of them is consistently available for the children. Saoirse's work is particularly adaptable: much of her composition, acoustic modeling, and installation design can be done from her home studio. Field research, performances, and collaborative installations are scheduled around the family's needs.

Saoirse's contribution to the household is impossible to miss. She fills it with music, experiments with interactive sound features, and is always finding new ways to make ordinary family activities more entertaining. Her sisters occasionally have to remind her that a quiet afternoon is not an invitation to test a new subwoofer.

She also learns to appreciate the value of silence. Raising children with two very different sisters teaches her that a supportive home needs room for exuberance, creativity, quiet reflection, and everything in between.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)

Saoirse's story is one of exuberant self-expression, artistic discovery, and chosen family. Growing up as one of three in a highly creative household, she learns to carve out her own space through sound. What begins as a child's fascination with music grows into a sophisticated exploration of acoustics, sensory perception, and the ways different forms of life experience their surroundings.

As an adult, she subverts traditional relationship expectations by choosing to build a permanent, co-mothering household with her sisters. Her career brings science and art together, extending the possibilities of cross-species interaction without losing sight of the emotional power of music.

She represents the joyful, noisy, interconnected future of New Eden—and discovers that sometimes the most meaningful sound is the one another person chooses to share with you.
