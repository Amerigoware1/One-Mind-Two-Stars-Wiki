## Overview
Saoirse Echo Keahi-Calder (pronounced *SEER-sha*) is the firstborn of the fraternal triplets carried by Maya and co-parented by Sam. Named for freedom (Saoirse) and sound (Echo), she inherits her parent Sam's deep love for audio engineering and artistic expression. She grows up running through the Samaya Gallery, becoming a bold, creative force who ultimately chooses to build a communal, co-parenting family with her two sisters.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Physical Description
Saoirse has Maya's warm Hawaiian complexion and Sam's striking, expressive eyes. Naturally blonde, she often dyes her hair wild colors (blues and purples) to match her vibrant personality. She dresses in eclectic, layered clothing, often adorned with audio equipment or sonic jewelry.
</div>

## Personality & Skills
Saoirse is defined by:
- **expressive, chaotic energy**
- **musical intuition**
- **fierce protectiveness**
- **unapologetic individuality**
Her strengths include:
- audio engineering and composition
- emotional communication through sound
- creative problem-solving
Her weaknesses include:
- impatience
- occasional sensory overload
- struggling to sit still in quiet environments

## Relationships
- **Maya Keahi** — mother; Saoirse admires Maya's visual art, often collaborating with her on mixed-media projects.
- **Sam Calder** — parent; Saoirse shares Sam's love of sound and fashion, and they frequently bond over music.
- **Kalea Lyric Keahi-Calder** — sister; Saoirse is the "loud" triplet, but she is fiercely protective of Kalea.
- **Indigo Nova Keahi-Calder** — sister; they share an unspoken understanding, often communicating through rhythms and beats.
- **The Trio's Children** — Saoirse raises her children collectively with Kalea and Indigo in a shared home.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Saoirse's story is one of exuberant self-expression and chosen family. Growing up as one of three in a highly creative household, she learns to carve out her own space through sound. As an adult, she subverts traditional relationship expectations by choosing to build a permanent, co-mothering household with her sisters. She represents the joyful, noisy, interconnected future of New Eden.
