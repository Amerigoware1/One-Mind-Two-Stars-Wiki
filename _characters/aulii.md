book1_title:  "Growing (age-8) "
book1_arc:  >
At age eight, Aulii doesn't appear in Book 1, but she's growing up in New Eden. She is Celectra's classmate in karate and other activities with their age cohort.
Aulii started karate when she was four years old and has progressed steadily. Now wearing a midori obi (green-belt), she is known among her instructors for thriving on the discipline and self-control that sundome (inch-stop) karate demands—a focus that surprises and impresses those who expect typical childhood restlessness.

book2_title: "The Gold Medal (age-15)"
book2_arc: >
At fifteen, Aulii competes in the Sundome Karate Tournament alongside Celectra Carisco. She faces Celectra in the finals, where Celectra is competing under the "stillness protocol," fighting with one body while the other remains motionless.
Aulii wins 4–1 through precise, disciplined technique, earning the Gold Medal while Celectra takes Silver.
After the match, Aulii demonstrates exceptional sportsmanship. She acknowledges the difficulty of Celectra’s constraint and offers a strategic suggestion; alternating bodies between matches to allow genuine recovery. This advice becomes a key adjustment for Celectra in future tournaments. Aulii’s victory is remembered not as an exploitation of limitation, but as a demonstration of excellence under fair conditions.

book3_title: "Young Instructor (age-22)"
book3_arc: >
Off-page, at twenty-two, Aulii begins assisting Sensei Kisa at the Sundome Dojo. She focuses on adapting traditional martial arts techniques for the unique physiologies of colony-born children, including those with sensory sensitivities or physical differences. She maintains a friendly rivalry with Celectra, sparring occasionally to keep her skills sharp.
She also begins collaborating with the Resonance Stewardship Program, designing physical training modules that incorporate rhythm and body awareness—skills that will later prove essential for the twins’ generation. Her reputation grows as a teacher who emphasizes precision over power, and respect over dominance.

book4_title: "Mentor to the Next Generation (age-30)"
book4_arc: >
At thirty, Aulii becomes Physical Training Lead for the youth cohort. She works closely with Duška Santoso Carisco (resonance-design) and Kael (acoustic rhythm) to create integrated training programs. When the twins—Jenni and Xara—begin formal physical education, Aulii becomes their primary instructor.
She is also the assistant Sensei and recognizes their strengths early; Xara’s analytical precision and Jenni’s intuitive body awareness. She tailors their training accordingly, teaching Xara defensive positioning and Jenni adaptive movement. Her lessons emphasize that physical discipline is not about fighting, but about understanding one’s own body and respecting others.

book5_title: "The Twins’ Instructor (age-37)"
book5_arc: >
At thirty-seven, Aulii is a senior instructor in the Resonance Stewardship Program. She prepares the twins (age-14) for field work, teaching them defensive tactics, situational awareness, and how to move safely in unstable environments such as caves and resonance chambers.
During the Resonant Echo rescue preparation, Aulii ensures the twins are physically equipped for the mission, even though they are officially “observers.” She teaches them how to fall, how to brace, and how to trust their bodies under stress. Her training proves crucial when the twins navigate the gallery rescue site.
After the rescue, Aulii helps integrate acoustic movement patterns—learned from Kael—into the standard martial arts curriculum. Her arc culminates in becoming a bridge between generations; trained by Sensei Kisa, she now trains the twins, ensuring that discipline, respect, and adaptability remain core values of New Eden’s physical culture.

book6_title: "The Student of Stone (age-44)"
book6_arc: >
At forty-four, Aulii steps back from daily dojo instruction to pursue a lifelong project hinted at in her youth; learning to read the Galinstanian "Memory Veins." Her decades of sundome (inch-stop) martial arts have given her a profound mastery of physical stillness and internal quiet. While others map the active resonance network, Aulii learns to quiet her own biological "noise" enough to receive the ancient, lithic history written in the bismuth fractals. She becomes the expedition's designated Lithic Resonance Translator.

book7_title: "The Lithic Translator (age-51)"
book7_arc: >
Aulii joins the Global Planetary Survey Team aboard *The Wayfarer*. Her role is critical; when the team encounters isolated, ancient Galinstanian clusters, Aulii is the one who interfaces with the deep-crust Memory Veins to establish trust. She proves to these ancient beings that humanity respects their ancestral archive, not as a resource to be mined, but as a history to be honored. Her daughter Mei, partner Samir, and grandchildren Sora and Nia are vital members of the expedition, making the rover a true multi-generational, chosen-family home.

book8_title: "The Living Archive (age-58)"

Aulii achieves a major breakthrough, successfully translating a complex ancestral resonance-pattern that reveals the location of a hidden, stable harmonic nexus. She becomes the foremost human expert on Galinstanian deep history. Her quiet, grounded presence is the emotional bedrock of the expedition, especially when the wilderness pushes the crew to its limits. She helps draft the preliminary "Lithic Accords," legally protecting the Memory Veins from future exploitation.

book9_title: "Legacy of Stillness (age-65)"

## Overview
Aulii Vongphachanh is a New Eden-born martial artist, former senior dojo instructor, and the expedition's Lead Lithic Resonance Translator. Slightly older than Celectra's generation, she is best known for her surgical precision, strategic calm, and the profound physical stillness she cultivated over decades of sundome karate. By Book 7, she leverages this mastery of internal quiet to safely interface with the fragile Galinstanian "Memory Veins," acting as the vital diplomatic bridge between humanity and the ancient, lithic archives of the native clusters.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Physical Description
Aulii carries herself with the quiet, unshakeable precision of a lifelong martial artist. She has light olive skin (4) with a warm undertone, and dark, straight hair of medium thickness, which she keeps in a tight, practical bun during field operations. Her dark brown, almond-shaped eyes feature a subtle epicanthic fold, holding a calm, deeply observant gaze. She stands at 175.7 cm with an athletic, perfectly aligned posture. Even in her fifties, her movements are economical and deliberate. She favors practical, layered expedition gear over traditional gi, though she still wears her black belt with understated pride.

## Personality & Skills
Aulii is defined by her **surgical precision, strategic calm, and profound receptive stillness**. 
Her strengths include:
*   **Lithic Resonance Translation:** Her mastery of internal quiet allows her to interface with the Memory Veins without projecting her own biological "noise," making her the only human capable of safely reading ancient Galinstanian history.
*   **Tactical Analysis & Field Safety:** Unmatched ability to assess environmental hazards and keep the expedition team physically safe in unstable wilderness terrain.
*   **Mentorship:** A deeply supportive, demanding, but fair teacher who pushes others to find their own physical and mental discipline.
Her weaknesses include:
*   Occasional over-formality that can seem distant to the more expressive members of the crew.
*   A tendency to internalize stress, relying on her "stillness protocol" rather than verbally asking for help when overwhelmed.

## Relationships
*   **Samir** — Mei's queerplatonic life partner, adoptive co-mother to Sora and Nia, and Chief Pilot/Logistics Coordinator of *The Wayfarer*. Aulii accepts and loves Samir as a son.
*   **Mei Vongphachanh** — daughter; Expedition Botanist and Hydroponics Lead. An aromantic, heterosexual woman who actively chose motherhood. She engaged in consensual, transparent encounters with willing single men (Pedro and Diomid) who acted solely as genetic donors with no parental responsibility, allowing Mei and Samir to raise the children as a unified, loving parental unit.
*   **Sora & Nia** — grandchildren; Sora (biological father: Pedro) is Jasper's exact age and closest wilderness cohort, while Nia (biological father: Diomid) is a playful, curious companion to Jasmine. They grow up in the back of the mag-lev rover, learning to navigate Ares alongside the Carisco-Bowers twins.
*   **Jenni & Xara Carisco-Bowers** — former students turned respected colleagues. Aulii is immensely proud of how the twins have grown, and she relies on their unique resonance skills to protect her while she interfaces with the deep-crust veins.
*   **Sensei Onishi Kisa** — mentor and predecessor; Aulii credits Kisa for teaching her that martial arts is not about fighting, but about understanding one's own body and respecting others.

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Aulii’s arc is about the evolution of discipline. What began as physical control in the dojo becomes, in her forties and fifties, a profound spiritual and diplomatic tool. Her ability to achieve absolute internal stillness makes her the key to unlocking the Galinstanians' ancient history. Her journey on the Global Survey Team is not just about translating the past, but about ensuring that the next generation—represented by her grandchildren and the Carisco-Bowers twins—inherits a world where that past is respected and protected.

## Key Moments
*   **The First Stillness (Book 6):** Aulii successfully quiets her own biological resonance enough to receive a coherent, non-linguistic "memory" from a shallow bismuth outcropping, proving her theory that martial stillness is the key to lithic translation.
*   **The Expedition Departure (Book 7):** Aulii boards *The Wayfarer* alongside her daughter Mei, Mei's partner Samir, and grandchildren Sora and Nia, officially taking on the role of Lead Lithic Resonance Translator for the Global Survey Team.
*   **The Ancient Accord (Book 8):** Aulii interfaces with a deeply isolated, ancient Galinstanian cluster. By projecting absolute, respectful stillness, she earns their trust, allowing the team to access a hidden harmonic nexus that saves the expedition from a catastrophic resonance storm.
*   **The Dojo's Elder (Book 9):** Retiring from active field duty, Aulii returns to New Eden to formally codify the "Lithic Accords" and pass her legacy of receptive stillness to the next generation of stewards.

## Themes
*   **Stillness as Strength:** True power is not in force, but in the profound, receptive quiet required to truly listen.
*   **Chosen Family:** Celebrating diverse family structures, including aromantic identity and queerplatonic co-parenting, as equally valid and deeply loving.
*   **Legacy of Respect:** Protecting the past not by conquering it, but by approaching it with humility and discipline.
*   **The Multi-Generational Expedition:** The idea that saving the world is not a young person's solo mission, but a collaborative effort spanning grandparents, parents, and children.

## Trivia
*   Aulii was the first person to suggest that the fractal patterns in the bismuth network could be "read" like a topological record, a theory she first pitched to Celectra as a teenager.
*   Sora's biological father is Pedro, and Nia's is Diomid. Both men were willing, transparent genetic donors with no parental responsibilities, reflecting Ares' progressive, stigma-free approach to family building. Mei and Samir are the children's only parents.
*   Her grandchildren, Sora and Nia, are the only ones who can successfully make her break her "stillness protocol" and laugh out loud, usually by attempting (and failing) to mimic her perfect martial stances.
*   She refuses to wear ANI interfaces in the deep caves, as the electronic "hum" disrupts her ability to achieve the absolute internal quiet required for lithic translation.
*   Mei named her firstborn, Sora, after the word for "sky" in their ancestral language, a nod to the fact that the child was born under the open, twin skies of Ares, not in a colony dome.
