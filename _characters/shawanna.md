### Overview
Shawanna Morris is a lifelong resident of Utopis with an adventurous spirit grounded by scientific rigor. From a young age, she has been drawn to the physical reality of their new world, preferring the tangible reality of rocks and minerals to the abstract complexities of colony politics. She is destined to become a leading exogeologist, dedicating her life to understanding the asteroid they call home.

</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

### Physical Description
Shawanna has an athletic, sturdy build, developed from years of physical training for zero-G and high-gravity environments. Her hands are often calloused and occasionally stained with mineral dust, a badge of honor she wears proudly. She has a habit of pushing her sweat-dampened hair out of her face with the back of her wrist. Her eyes are sharp and constantly scanning her environment, assessing structural integrity and rock formations even in casual settings.

</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

### Personality & Skills
Shawanna is defined by:
- deep curiosity about the physical and geological world
- a hands-on, practical approach to problem-solving
- adventurous spirit balanced by methodical scientific rigor
- comfort in isolation and harsh environments
- a straightforward, no-nonsense communication style

Her strengths include:
- exceptional spatial awareness and physical coordination
- expertise in mineralogy, subsurface mapping, and structural analysis
- resilience and adaptability in high-stress field conditions
- ability to work independently for extended periods

Her weaknesses include:
- occasional impatience with bureaucratic or administrative delays
- can be overly blunt, unintentionally offending more sensitive colleagues
- tendency to prioritize her work over her personal relationships

</div>

- **Xan Carisco & Ananya Patel** — friends from her youth
- **Utopis-Lab 3 Crew** — her second family, a tight-knit group of scientists and engineers who share her passion for the rock

### Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Shawanna’s arc is about exploration and grounding. While others look to the stars or the complex social dynamics of the colony, Shawanna looks down, into the ancient rock of Utopis. Her journey from a curious child watching Celectra in a zero-G gym to a seasoned exogeologist leading deployments on Utopis-Lab 3 is a testament to the human drive to understand and master their environment. She represents the practical, physical foundation upon which the colony's future is built.
