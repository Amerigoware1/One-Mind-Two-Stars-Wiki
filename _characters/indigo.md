## Overview
Indigo Nova Keahi-Calder is the youngest of the triplets. Named for the deep color (Indigo) and the stars (Nova), she represents the perfect synthesis of Maya's visual artistry and Sam's technical acoustic engineering. Highly analytical and fiercely intelligent, she grows up taking apart the gallery's sound nodes, and eventually dedicates her life to advancing technological bridges, all while raising her children in a collective family with her sisters.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Physical Description
Indigo has a striking appearance, often accentuating her atrichia (inherited from Sam) with bold, geometric makeup or intricate, tech-enhanced headpieces. She favors sharp, structural clothing in dark colors, punctuated by the glowing lights of the datapads she is constantly carrying.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Personality & Skills
Indigo is defined by:
- **relentless curiosity**
- **analytical brilliance**
- **fearless innovation**
- **stubborn independence**
Her strengths include:
- interface engineering and signal processing
- blending artistic design with hard science
- rapid problem-solving
Her weaknesses include:
- impatience with red tape
- hyper-fixating on problems
- sometimes forgetting to eat or sleep when immersed in a project
</div>

## Relationships
- **Maya Keahi** — mother; Indigo appreciates Maya's grounding presence, often using her mother's art as a visual anchor when testing new interfaces.
- **Sam Calder** — parent; Indigo shares Sam's analytical mind and technical skill, though she applies it in a more formal, scientific direction.
- **Saoirse Echo Keahi-Calder** — sister; Indigo relies on Saoirse's emotional intuition to understand the human element of her technical projects.
- **Kalea Lyric Keahi-Calder** — sister; Indigo is Kalea's biggest fan, often commissioning her sister's art to decorate the labs.
- **The Trio's Children** — Indigo raises her children collectively with Saoirse and Kalea in a shared home.
</div>
<div class="card-bg" markdown="1">

## Story Overview (non-spoiler)
Indigo's story is about pushing the boundaries of what is possible. Born into a world where the foundational work has already been done, she takes the legacy of her parents and the original bridge-builders and evolves it. She, along with her sisters, proves that the future of New Eden is bright, interconnected, and endlessly creative—both in the technology they build and the non-traditional families they nurture.
