📖 The "One Mind, Two Stars" Ennealogy

A nine-book generational sci-fi epic spanning nearly sixty years on Ares, tracing humanity's expansion, the indigenous Galinstanians, and an evolving interspecies symbiosis.

Arc I: The Bridge Generation (2232–2246)

  • Book 1: One Mind, Two Stars: The Bridge of Ares (Ready for Professional Edit): Introduces the Niamh–Ciara system, Utopis Station, Celectra's disoma physiology, and first contact.
  • Book 2: One Mind, Two Stars: The Northern Shadows (In Revision): Deepens political friction and ethical dilemmas surrounding sentient experimentation.
  • Book 3: One Mind, Two Stars: Neutron Legacy (In Revision): Arc I climax resolving immediate threats to Celectra and the Ares Colony.

Arc II: The Resonance Generation (2255–2269)

  • Book 4: Two Stars, One Destiny: The Four Rivers Resonance (Outline Complete, 2255): 7-year-old twins Jenni and Xara uncover harmonic fractures beneath river settlements.
  • Book 5: Two Stars, One Destiny: The Resonant Echo (Outline Complete, 2262): At age 14, Jenni and Xara decode an ancient acoustic signal at Samaya Gallery.
  • Book 6: Polar Opposites (Working Title) (Outlined, 2269): Young adult twins take separate polar paths with Chime and Ripple.

Arc III: The Wayfarer Expeditions (2276–2290)

  • Books 7–9: The Deep-Crust Frontier (Series Bible & Cast Established): Jenni, Xara, partners, children (Jasmine and Jasper), and Aulii's descendants traverse Ares's deep-crust Memory Veins aboard The Wayfarer exploration rover.
